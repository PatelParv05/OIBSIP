## Introduction

Network security threats can affect the confidentiality, integrity,
and availability of systems, applications, and data. Modern networks
connect users, cloud services, applications, IoT devices, and
critical infrastructure, creating a large attack surface. Threats
such as Distributed Denial of Service (DDoS), Man-in-the-Middle
(MITM), IP spoofing, and DNS poisoning can disrupt services,
intercept communications, redirect users, or hide the true source
of malicious traffic. Understanding how these attacks work and
applying appropriate preventive and detective controls are therefore
important responsibilities for network administrators.
